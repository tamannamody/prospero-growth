FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Which platforms do you work with?

Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Amazon, Flipkart, Instagram and Facebook. We recommend the ones that fit your product and margin, not all of them.

How long does it take to go live?

Usually about 30 days if your papers are ready. Approval timelines depend on the platform and your category.

What do I need before we start?

GST, PAN and a bank account. Barcodes for most platforms, and FSSAI if you sell food. Missing something? We help you sort it out.

Do you guarantee sales?

No honest agency can. We promise clear work, fast action and a weekly report in rupees, so you always know what is working.

Who owns my accounts and content?

You do. Seller accounts, ad accounts and creatives are in your name. If we part ways, everything stays with you.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. 15 minutes, a one-page report and 3 quick wins you can use even if you never hire us.
